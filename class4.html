Ch 03 HCF and LCM
Factors ▶

Find Highest Common Factor (HCF) and Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) of numbers.

Factors & MultiplesPrime NumbersHCFLCM

🧮 Formulae / Key Expressions

HCF = Largest factor common to both numbersLCM = Smallest common multipleHCF × LCM = Product of two numbers

📌 Key Points

  • Factors divide a number exactly
  • Multiples are in the times tables
  • HCF is used to simplify fractions
  • LCM is used to add unlike fractions

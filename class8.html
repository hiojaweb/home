Ch 01 Algebraic Identities
Algebra ▶

Standard algebraic identities and their applications in simplification.

(a+b)²(a-b)²(a+b)(a-b)(x+a)(x+b)Factorisation

🧮 Formulae / Key Expressions

(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²(a-b)² = a² - 2ab + b²(a+b)(a-b) = a² - b²(x+a)(x+b) = x² + (a+b)x + ab

📌 Key Points

  • Identities hold for all values of variables
  • Use identities to expand and factorise
  • (a+b)² ≠ a² + b² (common mistake!)
  • Memorise all 4 standard identities

Fractions ▶

Understand halves, quarters and thirds. Read and write fractions as ½, ¼, ⅓.

Half (½)Quarter (¼)One-Third (⅓)Parts of a Whole

🧮 Formulae / Key Expressions

Fraction = Numerator / Denominator½ means 1 part out of 2 equal parts

📌 Key Points

  • Numerator is the top number
  • Denominator is the bottom number
  • ½ is greater than ¼
  • A whole = all equal parts together
